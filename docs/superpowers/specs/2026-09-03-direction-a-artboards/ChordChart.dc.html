Form & Structure

ABCD, 36 bars total (8-8-8-12). Final A includes a 4-bar tag. Harmony moves through a chain of ii-V-I progressions with modulatory shifts (including major-third relations) that tonicize multiple centers before returning to the home key; common analyses note tonicizations of Ab and C in the A sections and a bridge that moves to distant keys before resolving back to Ab. Some lead sheets omit or shorten the tag.

Key centers: Ab→C (A), Eb→G (B), G→E (C/bridge), back to Ab (D). The B section transposes A down a perfect 4th. Bar 24's C7b13 functions as V7/vi returning home. Bar 32 uses Bdim7 as a passing diminished (iii°7). Final 4-bar tag (33–36) is a ii-V-I turnaround with the last bar setting up the next chorus. Common subs: tritone sub Db7 for G7 (bar 6); #IVdim7 passing chord in bar 32; altered dominants throughout. Charlie Parker's 'Bird of Paradise' is a famous contrafact on these changes.
